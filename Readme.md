## Premise of the Game

You start as a Nobody in the distant future in a world toiled by the hands of techno-feudalism, driven to ruin by Greed and Corruption.
Crime is one of the Ways out of your poor existence and you start by pickpocketing and begging and eventually rise up to do bank heists, hack the biggest tech-firms and recruit your own militia as a infamous mob-boss.
Cybernetic Enhancements and Futuristic Weaponery aid you on your Path to Riches. **Who will you become in this World?**

## Currently Implemented Features
* **World Generation** on three levels, Continentwide, Citywide and the actual Level. Using Perlin-Noise and Voronoi-Diagrams.
* **Basic Menus** and a Loading Screen.
* **Tilebased Movement** similar to Old School Roguelikes, like CDDA.
* **Chunk Loading** with smooth transitions between Chunks.
* **1-bit Aesthetic** reminding of 80s Computer Graphics.

## How the Sauce is made:
* **Engine:** Godot
* **Art:** Aseprite

### Keybinds
<pre>
- Movement (Player, Cursor):    WASD, Arrow keys, Numpad (also diagonal movement)
- Zoom:                         z (in), Z (out)
- Switch to continent map:      c
- Switch to region map:         m
- Switch to local:              l
- Use cursor:                   x (start/exit)
</pre>
